## Two-line section titles
The English pattern "small word / bold word" is kept as two Arabic lines that read as one phrase
(e.g. أقسام / الشركة, لماذا تختار / زمام, كلمة / رئيس مجلس الإدارة).

## Proper names
Client, project and partner names stay in English unless an official Arabic name is printed on the
company's own logo (see `assets/clients`): هابكو (HAPCO), ملتي كوت (Multicoat),
شركة الدوحة الحديثة للمقاولات (DMCC). Everything else (SEVEN, Elite, Kidzink, NHC, Shapoorji Pallonji,
Concrete Plus, ACME, Al Azaem, Raffles Hotel, KidZone, STC, Gulf Speaking Contracting Company,
Nexify Logistics) stays in Latin letters.
